# Healthcare.com

> Healthcare.com (HealthCare, Inc.) is a privately held, non-government health insurance
> marketplace and distribution platform founded in 2014. Consumers use it to find, compare
> and enroll in ACA/under-65 individual health plans, Medicare Advantage and Medicare
> Supplement plans, and specialty coverage. Distributors, FMOs, MGAs and agents use its
> Autopilot machine-learning enrollment acquisition platform, which matches high-intent
> shoppers to distributor partners with predictive scoring and value-based lead pricing.
> Its Pivot Health subsidiary develops proprietary short-term medical, fixed indemnity and
> supplemental gap products with A-rated carriers. Insurance is sold through
> Healthcare.com Insurance Services, LLC. Not affiliated with HealthCare.gov.

## APIs

Healthcare.com publishes **no public developer API** as of 2026-08-01: no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDKs, no MCP server and no A2A
agent card were found on any company host. `api.healthcare.com` is a live but private
first-party API host (root returns HTTP 418 with an empty body; unknown paths return a
plain-text 404) with no published documentation. See
`well-known/healthcare-com-well-known.yml` for the full probe record.

## Products

- **Autopilot** — machine-learning enrollment acquisition platform for health insurance distributors; predictive modeling, outcome-based pricing, branded customer handoffs, and Autopilot Voice.
- **Pivot Health** — proprietary direct-to-consumer specialty insurance (short-term medical, fixed indemnity, gap coverage).
- **Marketplace** — consumer plan comparison and enrollment across ACA/under-65, Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement.
